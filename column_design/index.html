Anchor layout: set one shared centre-to-edge distance for the symmetric pattern, or drag any blue anchor for a local adjustment. The coordinate readout and collision state update live.

Clearance: the default 25 mm centre distance leaves 15 mm metal beyond a Ø20 hole, or 12.5 mm beyond a Ø25 hole. Verify anchor edge distances, washer dimensions, concrete breakout, welds, and structural loads with the responsible engineer.
